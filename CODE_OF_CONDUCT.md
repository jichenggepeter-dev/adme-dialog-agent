# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community
for people of every background, identity, level of experience, and area of
expertise.

## Expected behavior

Community members should:

- be respectful and constructive;
- explain disagreements with evidence and without personal attacks;
- give and receive feedback in good faith;
- acknowledge uncertainty, mistakes, and the limits of their expertise;
- protect private information, credentials, molecular data, and reports;
- respect the different responsibilities of scientific and engineering
  contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized conduct;
- insulting, demeaning, or deliberately disruptive communication;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- pressuring contributors to disclose confidential research data;
- knowingly presenting unsupported scientific claims as established facts;
- using project spaces to promote unsafe, clinical, or deceptive uses of the
  software.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions,
documentation, project events, and other spaces where someone represents the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report sensitive conduct incidents in a public issue. Use the
repository's private reporting channel described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), and identify the report as a Code of Conduct
matter.

Maintainers will review reports privately and may edit or remove content,
close contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently remove someone from project spaces. Reporters' privacy will be
protected to the extent reasonably possible.

This policy is informed by the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
